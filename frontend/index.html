Workflow

Four steps, from source papers to a graded attempt.

1
Add papers

Upload Cambridge question papers with their mark schemes, or reference documents and patterns for AI generation.
2
Review the bank

Every question is cropped automatically. Check what was extracted and any warnings.
3
Build a set

Pick questions to a marks target and topic, or generate new ones from a pattern. Print it.
4
Grade

Upload photos of the completed sheet and get marks and feedback per question.
