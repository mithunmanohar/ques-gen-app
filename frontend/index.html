Welcome

A local tool for building practice question sets — either by re-using real Cambridge past-paper questions (cropped straight from the PDFs, diagrams and official mark schemes included) or by having DeepSeek write fresh, pattern-matched questions — and grading your child's photographed answer sheets against them.

🗂️

1. Admin

Upload Cambridge question papers + mark schemes to fill the question bank, or reference documents and exam blueprints ("patterns") for AI generation.

✨

2. Generate

Build sets from the bank by marks and topic, or generate brand-new questions that follow a pattern exactly.

📝

3. Submit & Evaluate

Print a set for your child to solve on paper, then upload a photo of the completed answer sheet to get it graded.
