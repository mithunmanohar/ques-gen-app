Welcome

A local tool for turning CBSE past-year papers into fresh, pattern-matched practice question sets — and grading your child's photographed answer sheets against them using DeepSeek.

🗂️

1. Admin

Upload past-year question papers (PDF/Markdown/text) and define the exam blueprint ("pattern") — sections, question types, marks, counts.

✨

2. Generate

Pick a pattern and reference material, and generate one or more new question sets that follow the blueprint exactly.

📝

3. Submit & Evaluate

Print a set for your child to solve on paper, then upload a photo of the completed answer sheet to get it graded.
