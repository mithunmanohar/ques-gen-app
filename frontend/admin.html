Cambridge past papers → question bank

Upload a Cambridge (CAIE) question paper PDF and, ideally, its mark scheme. Every question is cropped out automatically as an image (diagrams included), its marks are read, the mark scheme's answers are attached, and topics are tagged. Automatic checks flag anything that looks off — flagged questions are kept but left out of new sets unless you ask for them. Download the PDFs yourself (e.g. from your school or Cambridge's site); the app never fetches them.

Reference documents for AI generation

Upload PDF, Markdown, or plain-text past papers or notes. Text is extracted automatically and used as reference material when DeepSeek writes brand-new questions from a pattern. (Scanned/image-only PDFs won't extract well — see README.)
